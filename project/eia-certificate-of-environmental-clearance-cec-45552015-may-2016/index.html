EIA & Certificate of Environmental Clearance CEC 4555/2015 (May 2016)

Project Overview

Conducted an Environmental Impact Assessment & Project Managed the acquisition of the necessary Statutory Environmental Clearances for CARMINCO’s proposed Sand & Gravel Quarry Project. To support the CEC Application for the project we formulated and prepared the project’s Environmental Management & Monitoring Plans; Rehabilitation Plans; Stormwater Management Plans & Mine Designs. Additionally, air, noise and water quality testing were conducted to establish environmental baselines for the project.
