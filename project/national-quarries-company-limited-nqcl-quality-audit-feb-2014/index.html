National Quarries Company Limited (NQCL) Quality Audit – Feb 2014

Project Overview

The Client retained the services of Terran Resources to conduct a review of its operations with the aim of resolving quality issues plaguing its aggregate products, in particular its concreting sand product. To this end an audit of its Laboratory and operations processes was commissioned. Upon completion of the audit our services were retained to conduct critical training of Laboratory Staff to support the resolution of the issues identified.
