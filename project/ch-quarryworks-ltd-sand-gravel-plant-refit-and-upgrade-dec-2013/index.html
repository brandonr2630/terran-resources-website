C&H Quarryworks Ltd. – Sand & Gravel Plant Refit and Upgrade (Dec 2013)

Project Overview

The Client owns what was once a derelict sand & gravel washing and screening plant. Terran Resources was retained to conduct an assessment of the plant to guide the clients investment strategy. On the basis of the assessment the client was able to access the financing to carry out a re-fit of the plant. Upon commencement of the re-fit Terran Resources was retained to provide the necessary project supervision.
